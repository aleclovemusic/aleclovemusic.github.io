About Alec Love

.

Alec Love (he/him) is a student composer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Music in Composition at Central Michigan University. His journey in music began with early piano lessons, later expanding to guitar and drums during high school. Music quickly became a vital part of his life—one he couldn’t ignore.

Composing for his high school marching band sparked a deeper interest in writing for instrumental ensembles, which in turn fueled his growing passion for storytelling through music. Influenced by powerful scores in film and video games, Alec found his creative direction in media composition.

In addition to his studies, Alec brings a technical edge to his work through an Associate degree in Engineering/Physics from Lansing Community College. He currently serves as the Senior Staff Coordinator in the Audio/Visual Department at the CMU School of Music, where he combines his artistic and technical skills to support live production and recording.
